Lunch General Rules

  • Regular Season is divided into 6, 6, and 6 weeks. Referred to as periods.
  • The record (6, 6, and 6 weeks) of each team will be tracked for each period.
  • The TEAM with the best record for each period will get 20 CBs from the pot. (NOTE: The top team cannot have a losing record and receive 20 CBs from the pot).
  • 20 CBs is to cover their lunch during the team lunch event.
  • The winner(s) will be responsible for picking the date and time of the event.
  • Winning teams have 3 weeks to HOLD a lunch event after the last game of the period. Failure to HOLD the lunch event within this 3 week period will result in forfeit of 20 CBs.
  • If a winner undergoes more than 1 week of travel during this 3 week period, an extra week will be authorized to schedule this lunch.
  • Tiebreakers for lunch if more than 1 team qualifies:
    1. Total point differential for period
    2. Coin-toss

“Bragging Rights”: General Rules and Tiebreaking Procedures

  • The team that wins the Super Bowl will split the entire amount in the “POT” and have all the glory and respect of a Super Bowl Champion. Team merchandise must be acquired prior to Saint Patrick’s Day of that year and put on display for the team to see. If team merchandise has not been purchased by the prescribed deadline, 50 CBs must go back in the pot.
  • If no team wins the Super Bowl, all money collected remains in the “POT” and will be utilized for the preseason kick off and rules discussion the following season.
  • 2 CBs goes in the “pot” for every regular season loss per person. 5 CBs goes in the “POT” for every regular season tie per person.
  • Switching of teams is not allowed. We do not tolerate fair weather fans and demand unwavering loyalty.
  • David M is the owner of the excel spreadsheet.
  • Pat G is the Treasurer.
  • Turd Ferguson Award (TFA) is given to the team that has the biggest point differential after it has been offset by the Vegas spread each week. In the event of a tiebreaker, a coin flip will determine the TFA recipient.
  • Henry E is the head of the TFA committee. If the TFA has not been awarded within precisely two weeks of the end of the last game of the week, Henry/Bengals will receive the TFA for that week.
  • The team that has received the most TFAs at the end of the year is obligated to purchase a rival’s team flag chosen by the non-losers and display it until the start of the following season.
  • Flag must meet minimum dimension of 12” by 12” and be displayed where clearly visible.
